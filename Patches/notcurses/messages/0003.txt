in: report bracketed-paste boundaries as events

Surface the bracketed-paste start/end sequences as distinct events rather
than letting the payload arrive as an indistinguishable stream of individual
keypresses. This lets a caller buffer a whole paste at once, which is the
difference between a large paste being instant and being replayed one
keystroke at a time.

Deliberately a smaller shape than the design sketch in upstream issue #2704:
no fbuf/paste_content field is added inside notcurses itself, only the
boundary events, leaving the buffering to the caller's own event loop.
