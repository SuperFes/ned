Spec examples upstream lists in failing.txt whose trees ned checked against
the CommonMark spec's own HTML: its expected trees there were sketches
without delimiter children. Example 538's `[ref]:` line is a shortcut link to
the inline grammar alone; the block grammar takes definitions first. 588
and 635 are fixed in ned's grammar.janet (an angle-bracket destination and a
quoted attribute value outweigh the HTML tags their bytes could also read as;
an attribute may have no value).
